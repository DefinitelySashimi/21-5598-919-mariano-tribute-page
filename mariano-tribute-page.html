Linus Torvalds: The Father of Linux and Git

A Timeline of Linus Torvalds' Life and Work

  • 1969 - Born in Helsinki, Finland.
  • 1988 - Enrolls at the University of Helsinki, where he begins to study computer science.
  • 1991 - While a student, he began a project that eventually became the Linux kernel, a free operating system core. He first announced the project in a Usenet post.
  • 1994 - Version 1.0 of the Linux kernel is released.
  • 1996 - Joins Transmeta, a chip design company, and moves to California.
  • 1997 - Graduates from the University of Helsinki with a master's degree.
  • 1999 - Torvalds leaves Transmeta to focus full-time on the Linux kernel development under the guidance of the Open Source Development Labs (OSDL).
  • 2005 - Creates the **Git** version control system to manage the development of the Linux kernel, after the company that provided the proprietary BitKeeper system withdrew its free license. Git quickly becomes the de facto standard for open-source projects.
  • 2012 - Inducted into the Internet Hall of Fame.
  • Present - Continues to serve as the project coordinator and 'benevolent dictator for life' of the Linux kernel.

"Talk is cheap. Show me the code." - Linus Torvalds
